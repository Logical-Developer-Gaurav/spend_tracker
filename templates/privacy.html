Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Service. It should be read alongside our Terms and Conditions. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the information you provide when creating an account, such as your name, email address, and password. We also store the financial data you enter into the app, including expenses, categories, and amounts. We may automatically collect limited usage data, such as login timestamps, to help operate the Service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and maintain your account, let you log and review your expenses, and improve the Service. We do not sell your financial data or personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your account password is stored using industry-standard hashing, and we take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access. However, no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not share or sell your personal or financial data to third parties, except where required by law or to comply with a valid legal process.

5. Cookies & Tracking

Spendly uses essential cookies to keep you signed in and maintain your session. We do not use cookies for advertising or third-party tracking purposes.

6. Your Rights

You can access, update, or delete your account and the data associated with it at any time. If you would like a copy of your data or wish to permanently delete your account, please contact us using the details below.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account and expense data for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, your data will be permanently removed from our systems within a reasonable period.

8. Children's Privacy

Spendly is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of Spendly after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on the Spendly website.
